Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 October 2026

This page is run by Joe Taylor as a personal project. It collects as little as it can while still letting visitors repair the text together.

What is stored

The shared state of the page, which is a record of how worn each character is, the total number of repairs ever made and the date the page was last fully intact. None of this identifies you.

To stop one person repairing the whole page at once, each visitor can make 20 repairs an hour. When you repair a word, your IP address is combined with a secret value and passed through SHA-256. Only the resulting hash and a count are stored, and they are deleted automatically within two hours. Your IP address itself is never written down.

Your browser keeps your answer to the cookie notice in local storage so the notice does not appear on every visit. It is not sent anywhere.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies of its own and uses no analytics, advertising or third-party tracking.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes requests on my behalf and may keep short-lived server logs for security and reliability. See Cloudflare's own privacy policy for details.

Your rights

Because nothing stored can be linked back to you, there is normally nothing to access or delete. If you have a question about this policy, you can contact me through taylortowe.rs.
